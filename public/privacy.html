Privacy Policy

yourparttimebiz.com · Last updated:

This policy explains what ("we," "us") collects when you use yourparttimebiz.com, why, and who it is shared with.

What we collect

  • The questionnaire: your name, your email address, and your answers to the 16 questions. Your answers can include personal details such as where you live, your work history and your finances. Share only what you're comfortable sharing.
  • Your results: the business ideas written for you.
  • The "Talk to us" form: your name, email address, phone number if you give one, and your message.
  • Technical details: your IP address is used to limit how often the questionnaire can be submitted. We keep it only as a scrambled value (a hash) alongside a daily count.

How we use it

  • To create your business ideas and show them to you.
  • To email you a copy of your results.
  • To review submissions and follow up with you about our setup services.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To prevent abuse of the site.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the companies that run the site for us:

  • Anthropic, the AI provider. Your name and your answers are sent to its AI model (Claude) to write your ideas. Your email address is not sent.
  • Netlify, which hosts the website, and holds a copy of a submission or message if our database cannot be reached.
  • Microsoft Azure, which stores submissions and messages in our database and delivers our emails.

If you ask about a partner service (website building, or a RADS10 listing), we pass your details to that partner only if you ask us to.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Saved on your device

While you fill out the questionnaire, your browser keeps a copy of what you've typed so a reload doesn't lose it. That copy stays on your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep submissions, results and messages for as long as they are useful for the purposes above, and delete them when you ask us to.
